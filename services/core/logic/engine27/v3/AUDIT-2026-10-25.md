2. Micro V1 neighbor compatibility requires same directional string; cannot distinguish ordinary corrective movement from genuine structural incompatibility.
3. Provisional working Micro owner levels mean nextObjective may remain null. Do not bypass provisional guard.
4. The two public Micro/current wave interpretations are exposed in parallel; provenance boundary must decide authority before V3.
5. Cross-market candidate events can be rendered as supportive/adverse, so V3 must preserve event confirmation strength.
6. Engine10 structure-at-entry can be missing by design; do not construct entry thesis from current structure.
7. Existing read-only flags are useful but require tests ensuring no production wiring or permission mutations.
