### TradingView documented behavior versus owner evidence

TradingView describes back-adjustment as shifting prior contracts by old/new daily-close differences near a switch, usually the preceding daily bars. Its switch rule is symbol-specific and statistically informed by volume, so a single observed crossover does not prove a switch. Settlement close is an optional daily-and-higher setting; intraday close is unaffected by that toggle. These documented mechanics do not establish the owner's chosen settings, the exact coefficient close convention, ES1! switch history or original vintage.
