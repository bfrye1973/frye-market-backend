## Reading conventions and conceptual foundation

The chapter separates analysis, a trading plan and management. A plausible analysis alone does not establish an executable plan or a successful outcome. An A/1 or B/2 label expresses alternative interpretations, even when their immediate directions agree. A drawn arrow is a forecast made at that pictured stage; the later bars in a subsequent figure are later evidence, not evidence available to the earlier count.

The p.22 definition describes a zigzag as a three-part correction whose A and C each have five subdivisions and whose B has three. The p.27 definition describes an expanded flat as three subdivisions in A, three in B, and five in C, with B extending beyond A's start and C beyond A's end. These internal requirements cannot be proven from just four outer pivots. The flat family must remain distinct from zigzags; a running triangle is also not a running flat. No universal numeric B-retracement threshold for a regular/running flat has been established by this partial source review.

Within a correction, 5 or 3 denotes structured lower-degree components, not five or three candles. Nor does 'three' mean every corrective child has exactly three raw pivot segments: a B triangle or a combination may require a different internal grammar. Any such composition must be validated as its own pattern. The coffee example explicitly has a triangle in a zigzag's B position.

Every historical fixture still needs authorized OHLC data, source timezone, exact bar timestamps, adjustment/roll policy and a recorded information cutoff. Screenshot labels support conceptual and arithmetic tests; they are not executable bar-by-bar datasets.

## TECH - expanded-flat hypothesis, delayed completion and protected exit

Case ID: `C2-TECH-2012`. Source pp.26-36, Figs 2.5-2.15; **pp.30-32 and Figs 2.9-2.11 absent**. Instrument Techne Corporation stock, 60-minute chart, February-April 2012. The short setup follows an earlier decline labeled A or 1. Inside the rebound, three waves up from 68.84 to 71.00 followed by three down to 67.69 permit an expanded-flat or running-triangle interpretation. The next advance has already developed four of the expected five motive waves, supporting an expanded-flat hypothesis. It is still a hypothesis.

The expanded flat here is the **upward corrective rebound**, with its internal a-b-c, inside a possible larger B or 2. The projected downward C or 3 is at the larger degree. A flat detector that mixes the larger A/1 with the rebound's internal a-b-c would assign the wrong direction and degree.

## Silver - nested context, multiple target formulas, missed early entry and gap

Case ID: `C2-SILVER-1998`. Source pp.44-59; **pp.56-57 and Figs 2.34-2.35 absent**. Sources include monthly/daily silver futures continuation charts and March 1998 pit-session 15-minute charts. The book uses **cents per ounce**: 650.0 means $6.50, not $650. Sentiment and the news account are attributed historical narrative; neither was independently researched here.

The monthly context is a double-three combination: W zigzag, X flat, Y expanded flat. Within Y, C is a developing impulse, and within that C the relevant local move is a fifth. This is not a double zigzag merely because both notations contain W-X-Y. The parent grammar must retain the child families.

The arithmetic projections use three anchors: a measured move's beginning and end plus a new origin. The two-anchor prototype's start-origin extension cannot directly express A=C from B or a fifth projected from 4. Do not quietly replace formulas to make them match a chart; record input precision and rounding policy.

This example also distinguishes an exchange/session price gap from a three-candle FVG, a target from a cap, and a counterfactual ideal entry from actual behavior. The narrative's psychological lesson is sticking with an analysis despite a news surprise, but this cannot become a software rule that news is irrelevant or that target hits guarantee reversals. R-19/20/21/22 cover units, multi-anchor formulas, counterfactual segregation, and missing execution records.
