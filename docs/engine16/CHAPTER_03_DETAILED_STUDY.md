## Core lesson: geometry, placement and confirmation are different evidence

The opening p.77 associates sideways movement and converging boundaries with a triangle. Those observations are recognition clues; they do not establish complete subdivisions, parent role, final E or breakout direction. In the gold example, the same contracting geometry can be a fourth wave in an uptrend or B within a larger correction. Each has a different projected subsequent move and different protective references. Price evidence must discriminate; a later correct direction cannot be attributed to an earlier unique count that did not exist.

The chapter repeatedly places standalone triangles in4/B/X and before the final actionary segment of the relevant sequence. 'Final' must be scoped to the named sequence/degree. A small fourth-wave triangle can precede only one local fifth while larger structures continue. A nested triangle inside E has its own child degree; that is not permission to assign a standalone triangle to any parent role without a grammar.

For Engine16, publish (a) geometry evidence, (b) structural family/child proof, (c) parent-role alternatives, (d) provisional or confirmed research completion, and (e) separately timed price-break observations. A trading plan is an example attached to a scenario, not output authority to execute it.

### p.91: invalidation hierarchy materially qualifies the prior chapter proposal

For contracting and barrier triangles, the prose distinguishes three scopes:

- Crossing C invalidates the **current terminal-E interpretation**, while the larger triangle may survive because C itself may still be developing.
- Crossing A invalidates the **whole triangle case** discussed there.
- A practical stop may be chosen at c/E according to risk tolerance; its trigger is neither a universal proof of parent invalidation nor a verified fill.

Engine16 must therefore attach `invalidatedEntityId` and `scope` to each failed predicate. The earlier Chapter2 C2-TRI-01/R-32 must not be implemented as unconditional rejection of the entire parent when E passes C. A revised current-endpoint hypothesis and immutable earlier interpretations can coexist.

## Derived programming implications, separated from author teaching

Represent each scenario as a parent/child graph with family, role, anchor provenance, as-of/known-at clocks, source revision and rule version. Changes to completion interpretation append a superseding version; observations and earlier saved outputs remain immutable. A family-specific failed predicate points to the failed entity and may leave the parent DEGRADED/DEVELOPING rather than REJECTED.

Keep five independent object types: structural hypotheses; geometric lines/measurements; target models; confirmation/invalidation observations; and illustrative trade plans/outcomes. Fibonacci confluence and channel agreement are evidence with explicit yes/no/unknown states, not proof of a count. Opposing scenarios share evidence only by ID, not by overwriting each other's internal labels.

Missing pages and unavailable raw bars limit numeric fixtures. The research can specify synthetic boundary tests, arithmetic and source-linked replay expectations now; actual historical replays require raw bars, exact sessions, contracts, corporate-action/roll policies and time cutoffs.
