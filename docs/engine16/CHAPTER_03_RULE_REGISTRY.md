## Crosswalk and qualifications of earlier proposed rules

- **C2-TRI-01 and R-32:** retain E-versus-C evidence but qualify the invalidation scope with C3-INV-01/02. p.91 supplies the important parent-survival distinction absent from the shorter Chapter2 excerpt. A candidate may need a new C/E segmentation rather than wholesale triangle-family rejection. No earlier code exists that this study claims to repair.
- **C1-TRI-02 / C1-CONF methods:** C3-CONF-01/02 supply concrete examples of fixed-extreme, range-break and trendline-break methods. These remain distinct triggers and trading preferences, not a single universal confirmation algorithm.
- **C2-TRI-02 / C2-NEST-01:** DELL gives a full nested-E explanation with separately identifiable parent/child widths, as-of revisions and trigger lines.
- **C2-FIB-W5-01:** C3-TGT-06 reuses the three-anchor measured-move/projection-origin model; it is not the prototype's two-anchor start-origin extension.
- **E16-TIME-01 / E16-OUT-01:** identical historical charts may be reinterpreted later. Immutable evidence and append-only scenario versions remain mandatory design proposals; source-time and market-time must also remain separate.
