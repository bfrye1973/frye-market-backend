## 1. What the chapter adds (p.99)

A zigzag has motive five-wave A, corrective three-wave B, then motive five-wave C. The preparatory five followed by three can identify a candidate continuation within the correction; three outer turns alone cannot verify internal subdivisions. This is stronger than the prototype's primitive three-swing ABC proposal. The chapter follows two trades Wayne Gorman reports actually making in 1998, including mistakes and losing reentries. Their reported fills deserve a different evidence type from Chapter 3's hypothetical midpoint transactions; neither has independently verified broker evidence here.

The setup can point down inside a larger upward structure. A local C direction is not the same as the parent trend, and the conclusion that C is developing does not establish its terminal price or elapsed time. Targets, channels, count validity and discretionary exits must remain separate concepts.

## 4. Subsequent S&P movement and losing reentry (pp.110–111)

Figure 4.11: later Friday price passed 927.4 and reached 926.0; Monday January 12 reached 917.7 and then rallied to 940.0. These observations occurred after the six-contract exit. They cannot be used as the initial trade's realized cover, or as information available when it was opened.

Figure 4.12: the larger triangle subsequently has C at 917.7 and D rising. The author reports another short into that rally while rationalizing that [4] of v was still developing; later assessment says [4] and [5] were already finished. Reentry lost $17,650, reducing the reported combined profit to $14,125. Exact reentry quantities, fills and stop events are not provided. Preserve a separate reported aggregate loss, not invented individual transactions. Regret about exiting early is the author's psychological explanation, not a deterministic market feature or a universal prohibition on reentry.

The chapter thus provides an important counterexample: a useful original directional idea and profitable first episode can be followed by an unprofitable reuse of the narrative after its supporting structure has ended. Require fresh evidence and candidate lineage in proposed research, subject to manager review.

## 7. Integration implications

Retain distinct parent role, local direction, structural validity, guideline quality, target status, lifecycle, and reported trade outcome. Targets must bind to three anchor IDs, ratio, quote basis and candidate version. Degree revision produces a new linked snapshot; it cannot mutate Engine 22's approved working structure. A channel and a Fibonacci projection sharing anchors are related evidence, not independent statistical confirmation. These examples motivate testable research contracts; they do not validate a deployable classifier.
